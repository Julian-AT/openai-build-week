- Evidence source closure covers the six recovery/replay production files, both production callers, their ten behavior test/verifier files, the three frozen schemas, all 23 capture fixture members, and the canonical active-generation inventory format.
- Verification uses a clean detached worktree at the exact recorded commit. It reruns every stored argv and validates stable semantic summaries, so unrelated current-worktree changes neither contaminate evidence nor waive dirty behavior-source rejection.
- The record is canonical one-line JSON plus one newline. Its `record_sha256` is recomputed with that field omitted, and unknown fields or any byte-level noncanonical form fail closed.
