<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Untrusted `.rrcap` bytes -> ArchiveVerifier | Room imagery metadata, JSON, paths, digests, and record relationships are attacker-controlled until both contracts and cross-record semantics pass. |
| VerifiedArchive -> ReplayCore | The capability is authority-bearing; construction and generation/member binding must not be forgeable or mutable. |
| Verified member descriptor -> filesystem read | Files can be substituted after admission, so consumption must re-establish exact-byte identity. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-02.1-01 | Tampering | ArchiveVerifier | high | mitigate | Task 1 applies pinned CON-001/CON-002 validation, cross-record checks, bounded safe paths, and rehashed adversarial cases before capability minting. |
| T-02.1-02 | Elevation of privilege | VerifiedArchive construction | high | mitigate | Tasks 1-2 keep initialization private to the sole verifier and remove all raw-path ReplayCore entry points. |
| T-02.1-03 | Tampering | Consumption-time member reads | high | mitigate | Task 2 bounded-reads and rehashes the exact generation member immediately before use. |
| T-02.1-04 | Information disclosure | Typed rejection surface | low | accept | Errors expose stable rejection classes and normalized member roles only; raw room bytes and host paths are never logged. |
</threat_model>
